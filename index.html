About me

I'm theoz, an independent Minecraft mod developer specializing in crafting realistic, high-fidelity systems using the Forge 1.20.1 platform.

I architect every layer myself: from custom 3D voxel models and PBR-inspired textures, to complex multi-packet client/server state machines and volumetric mathematical renderers. My goal is to make every feature feel natural and deeply integrated into vanilla Minecraft.

Beyond client mods, I also engineer custom server game modes and high-capacity networks, managing setups from scratch with thousands of unique player interactions.
